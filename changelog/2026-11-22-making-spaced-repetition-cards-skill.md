New [making-spaced-repetition-cards](https://github.com/rufuspollock/skills/blob/main/skills/making-spaced-repetition-cards/SKILL.md) skill: turns a book chapter, essay or wisdom text into an Anki deck of short cards with one clear answer each. The test is that an AI could score your reply against the back without doubt. It comes with a checker script and a judge prompt calibrated on real critiques of earlier decks. Cards carry stable ids and source links, such as Sutras passage ids, so decks can be regenerated without duplicates. In blind tests its decks passed the judge at 72 to 100%, against 0 to 29% for the earlier long-answer decks.
